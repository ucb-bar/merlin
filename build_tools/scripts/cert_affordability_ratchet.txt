# Capsules that demand L3 at a size over the affordability budget while declaring neither
# `max_oracle_tier: L2` nor `extends`. THIS FILE MAY ONLY SHRINK.
#
# Keyed `<target>/<capsule>`: a bare name is NOT unique -- SY_micro_model exists under two
# targets with different costs, and a bare key let one target's accepted debt excuse the other's.
#
# ⚠️ THE BUDGET THIS FILE IS TAKEN AT IS 300s, THE ONE THE CORPUS IS SIZED TO. It previously said 900,
# which is not a bar anything in the repo uses: every conformance spec on disk declares
# `cert_affordability.budget_s: 300.0` and sizes its members to the ~862-875 written elements that
# affords. Running the gate at the corpus's own budget against a ratchet taken at three times it
# reports debt growth that is really a moved bar -- 16 rows at 900s against 113 at 300s.
#
# Cost law from four cycle-accurate calibration runs (177.2/351.6/723.1/1682.6 s at
# 512/1024/2048/4096 written elements): seconds = 0.20509 * output^1.0782, log-log r2 0.9976.
# SUPERLINEAR, so a flat rate under-predicts by 16% at the top rung and 57% at the largest here.
#
# ⚠️ NOT EVERY REMEDY IS OPEN TO EVERY ROW, and the gate now says which are (`remedies` per row):
#   - a cycle-counted performance capsule cannot be capped without deleting its measurement;
#   - a target whose adapter registry offers only its cert tier has NO cheaper tier to cap onto, so
#     `max_oracle_tier` and `extends` are both unavailable to it. Two of the six roster targets are
#     in that position and they carry 45 of the over-budget rows.
# Both keep the other two remedies: a smaller shape, or an accepted cost recorded here with a reason.
