# Full Smol policy host admission

The full policy executes two vision images, 16 VLM prefix layers, and ten 16-layer expert/Euler updates: 1,421 ordered projection calls in 2,715 physical projection panels. The projection-only profile launches those 2,715 panels; the attention-inclusive full policy adds 200 attention launches, for 2,915 Atlas launches. `tools/smol_full_plan.py` fixes their geometry, dtype, scale scope, and ordering. Its 47 trajectory references cover prefix embeddings/masks/KV, step inputs/velocities/outputs/timesteps, and final state/actions.

The builder preserves the captured `atlas.full_kernel_assets.v1` manifest as `host/captured-kernels.json`, bound by digest with the unchanged replay and trajectory. Selected `kernels.json`, provenance, and contract agree on the launch programs. Captured fields stay identical except source/program paths, requested DMA, and selected arena.

Global policy is `captured-baseline-v1` or `qualified-reuse-v1`; each launch declares `baseline-v1` or `traffic-reuse-v1`. Baseline programs retain captured bytes or audited relocations. Reuse requires the public generator's exact source and DMA accounting and the pinned assembler's exact binary. Shapes outside the allowlist stay baseline.

`tools/host_iss/smol_full_contract.py` checks embedded panel/call descriptors, packed weights and instructions, learned/input/function parameters, writable control state, and distinct actual/reference storage. Tables and instruction/weight pointers need read-only file-backed load ranges. The input conversion buffer holds 3,145,728 BF16 words for the 1024-by-3072 vision FC2 predecessor; physical activation panels need at most 786,432 bytes. Three host load segments, a separate zero-file-size arena PT_LOAD, BSS, and the 64 KiB stack must fit the selected end-exclusive DDR range without overlap. Contract, provenance, and build placement agree.

`tools/build_smol_full_host.py` requires `--phase-reference ARCHIVE/phase-reference.json`, the authenticated fixed-observation boundary archive and referenced blobs, alongside captured kernels and trajectory. Every supported profile emits the production dual-mode driver: continuous and admitted single-layer modes share graph providers and parameter tables. New-observation boundary preparation is outside this fixed-capture workflow. Image, attention, normalization, activation, cache, greedy, and Euler operations produce live successor activations; trajectory arrays supply comparison references.

`tools/check_smol_full_bundle.py --bundle BUNDLE --model-root MODEL --out AUDIT.json` uses the pinned model's assembler in its compatible Python environment. Its `PASS_STATIC_ONLY` receipt distinguishes reassembled reuse programs from exact baseline relocations and checks bundle, ELF, contract, source, and model identities before and after reading. It executes neither guest nor accelerator code and constructs no model Core.

The continuous runner accepts `--workload smol-full` with an explicit wall budget and complete bundle. Pinned model requirements, device and final-buffer checks, segmented qualification, UART records, and FireSim packaging follow the shared [full-model contracts](full-model-contracts.md). Static preparation and continuous-runner support do not establish a completed numerical run.
