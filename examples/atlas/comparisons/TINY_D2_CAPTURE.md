The D2 C graph capture entry is d1-capture-c-v2.py. Its original47 projection worker and concurrent stream backfill drivers are in this directory. Set ATLAS_ROOT, MERLIN_ROOT, ATLAS_NPU_MODEL_ROOT, and TINY_WORK_ROOT to physical absolute paths, use one tensor thread per process, and use at most 16 workers. The full capture, export, qualification, and portable receipt workflow is documented in atlas-mlir tools/tiny_full/REPRODUCING.md. The driver requires external checksum-bound parameter and scale inputs, as listed by --help.
