The generator's `Q5_correctness_caps.dram_window_wrap` records that the
harness DRAM slave maps bytes with `address & ((1 << 20) - 1)`. It explicitly
warns that layer-scale tensors exceeding the 1 MiB window alias and can be
silently dropped unless the caller detects collisions.

`capture_bridge.validate_gsim_dram_window` now mirrors that integration limit
and rejects the unsliced image before execution. The qualified implementation
uses three independently checked N slices [0,256), [256,512), and [512,720).
This evidence records an integration constraint; it does not claim that Atlas
RTL itself has a 1 MiB architectural DRAM limit.
