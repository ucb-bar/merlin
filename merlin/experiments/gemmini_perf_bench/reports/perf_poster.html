Two axes, kept separate: cycle-accurate performance (verilator + FPGA) and correctness/capability (which backend compiles each op at all).

1 · Cycle-accurate performance

Cycles per kernel — generated (gold) vs hand-tuned golden (steel). verilator for small kernels, FireSim FPGA for the larger model/attention shapes (same RTL).

2 · Correctness & capability

Who actually compiles & runs each kernel (spike). The conv2d/movement rows are the differentiator: only merlin-gen (v1) lowers them.

3 · Why spike is not a performance number

spike is functional — its cycle count plateaus (~120) regardless of kernel size, giving impossible >100% utilization. Only RTL (verilator/FireSim) is valid timing.

Capability (spike, correct/total): golden 20 · baseline 17 · merlin-gen 24 · IREE 17 · native 17 (of 24). Generated backends (v0/v1/native) emit identical RoCC for matmul/attention → identical cycles; merlin-gen shown as representative.
