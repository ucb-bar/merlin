## groot-specific questions

**(a) Is there a denoise loop in source? K value? captured or config?**
Yes — a Python Euler `for t in range(self.num_inference_timesteps)` loop at `gr00t_n1d7.py:382`, with `K = num_inference_timesteps = 4` (config gr00t_n1d7.py:105). It is **NOT captured**: the loader (`Gr00tDenoiseStep`) runs exactly one step and the Euler update `actions = actions + dt*v` (gr00t_n1d7.py:421) never appears in FX/MLIR. K=4 survives **config-only** as `loop_count=4` in `MODEL_ARCH` (models.py:59) ⇒ `sidecar_or_config_only`.

**(b) Action horizon H source?**
`action_horizon = 40` (config gr00t_n1d7.py:75; `MODEL_ARCH(... 'denoise_steps', 4, 30.0, 16)` plus H carried in shapes). H is **preserved structurally**: `actions: f32[1,40,132]` is a graph input (groot_n1d7.txt:41); after `cat(state_features[1×1], action_features[1×40])` the DiT sequence is 41 (MLIR `…x41x…`, line 715), and the head returns `pred[:, -40:]`. M=40/41 appears throughout the attn/proj shapes ⇒ `present_and_preserved`.

**(c) Is the exported module one denoise step or the whole loop?**
**One denoise step.** Confirmed three ways: loader docstring ("One flow-matching denoise step"); FX has no `while/for/scan` (0 hits) and no Euler `add` of `dt*v`; only `transformer_blocks_0` and `transformer_blocks_1` appear as parameters (depth capped at 2 by `M2M_GROOT_LAYERS=2`, vs config's 16). So the capture is one step × 2 of 16 blocks — a smoke-sized slice, not the K=4 loop and not full depth.

**(d) prefix/backbone vs repeated action-head split — captured + attributed correctly or degenerate?**
The VLM **backbone is entirely out of graph** — it enters as the `backbone_features: f32[1,64,2048]` input (loader passes already-embedded features; docstring). So there is no in-graph prefix/backbone compute to split. Within the captured action head, Merlin's `region_role` attribution is **meaningful, not degenerate**: `backbone_once` (49 ops: state/action encoders, timestep embedder, pos-embed — things computed once per denoise call) vs `repeated_head` (110 ops: the per-block DiT attn/ff that would repeat across the 16 blocks / K steps), from `prov.fqn` (inventory). Note this `backbone_once` label refers to once-per-step setup, **not** the (absent) VLM backbone. Attribution is correct at the fqn level; the only defect is op-class mislabeling (see (e)).

**(e) ATTENTION RECOVERY CHECK (known gap) — VERDICT: groot is NOT hit by the known gap; Merlin DOES recover its attention, but over-counts it.**
- In `work_coverage_table.csv`: `n_attention_ops=11`, `attention_macs=354692096`, `total_recovered_macs=2966825984`, `visible_linear_fraction=0.8804`, `n_softmax=6`. So `n_attention_ops` is **non-zero (11)** — not the 0/low symptom of the gap.
- **Form groot's attention takes in flat MLIR:** the 2 `aten.scaled_dot_product_attention` (groot_n1d7.txt:26) are **decomposed** into two `linalg.generic` batch-matmuls per block carrying `prov.family="contraction", prov.op="batch_matmul"` (regions `matmul_11/12` for block 0, `matmul_20/21` for block 1; MLIR:715, region scan confirms `matmul_11` prov.op="batch_matmul"), plus first-class `prov.op="softmax"` regions (`softmax_0/softmax_1`, MLIR:746). **There is NO `prov.op="sdpa"` and NO `prov.family="attention"` anywhere** (grep count = 0 for both).
- **Why Merlin catches it:** `attribution.py:250` classifies `fam=="contraction" and prov_op=="batch_matmul"` (with valid M/N/K) as `OPC_ATTENTION="attention_contraction"`. groot's qkᵀ/attn·v are emitted in exactly that caught form, so they land as `attention_contraction` (inventory idx 80,89,122,129; shapes `41×48×64`, `41×64×48`, `41×48×41`, `41×41×48` @ batch=32 heads). The gap (an SDPA emitted as a single `linalg.generic` tagged `prov.op="sdpa"`/`family="attention"`, which `extract_non_gemm_ops` would miss because it only catches `family=contraction/op=batch_matmul`) **does not apply** to groot — groot's SDPA never takes that fused form.
- **The real defect (over-count, not miss):** of the 11 `attention_contraction` ops, only **4 are true attention** (the attn1 qkᵀ/attn·v, summing **13,224,960** MACs). The other **7** are the per-embodiment `CategorySpecificMLP`/`MultiEmbodimentActionEncoder` GEMMs — `torch.bmm(x, selected_W)` at embodiment_conditioned_mlp.py:79 — which also lower to `prov.op="batch_matmul"` and so trip the same classifier: `state_encoder.layer1/2`, `action_encoder.W1/W2/W3`, `action_decoder.layer1/2` (inventory idx 11,16,21,35,41,160,165). They contribute **341,467,136** of the 354,692,096 `attention_macs` (~96%). So `attention_macs` is dominated by per-embodiment **MLP** work mislabeled as attention; genuine attention is ~13.2M MACs (~3.7%).
- **Net verdict:** attention is **lowered (named op erased) but recovered** as MACs — `present_in_source_erased_by_lowering`. groot evades the known SDPA-generic gap. The actionable inaccuracy is the opposite: `attention_contraction` conflates true attention with embodiment-conditioned `bmm` MLPs because both carry `prov.op="batch_matmul"`; `n_attention_ops`/`attention_macs` are over-counted ~2.75× / ~27×.

## FINDINGS
- Source has a real K=4 Euler denoise loop (gr00t_n1d7.py:382) over a 16-block AlternateVLDiT (config:90); the **capture is one step of only 2 blocks** (`M2M_GROOT_LAYERS=2`), so loop, depth (16), Euler latent update, and repeated-weight lifetime are all `present_in_source_erased_by_export`, surviving only as config (`loop_count=4`, models.py:59).
- H=40 and dtype=f32 are cleanly preserved through FX→MLIR→artifacts. There is **no quant, no conv, no KV-cache, no scales/packed layout** in source — correctly absent (the VLM backbone is host-side, entering as the `backbone_features` input).
- **Attention-recovery verdict:** groot's 2 SDPAs decompose to `prov.op="batch_matmul"` + first-class `softmax` (no `sdpa`/`family="attention"` tag exists), so Merlin's caught form applies and attention **is recovered** (`n_attention_ops=11`≠0). groot does **not** suffer the known SDPA-as-generic miss.
- **But `attention_contraction` over-counts:** only 4 of 11 ops (13.2M of 354.7M MACs) are true attention; the other 7 are per-embodiment `CategorySpecificMLP` `torch.bmm` GEMMs (embodiment_conditioned_mlp.py:79) mislabeled because they share `prov.op="batch_matmul"`. Region/fqn attribution itself is correct (`backbone_once`/`repeated_head` from `prov.fqn`); the op-class taxonomy is the only defect.
- No `recaptures_levels/groot_n1d7/` exists (no high-level/qdq level MLIR for groot), consistent with a fp32, no-quant workload.
