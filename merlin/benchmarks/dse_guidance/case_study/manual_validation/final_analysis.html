Merlin DSE-Guidance — Final Analysis

Compiler-derived workload contracts for accelerator DSE · 11 VLA/VLM/LLM workloads · phases P16–P20 · source-grounded & verified

Thesis. Merlin is not a DSE optimizer. It recovers workload facts, constraints, mapspace seeds, capture-fidelity status, and HW/SW-boundary candidates from real model captures — useful to a future DSE tool — and refuses every quantitative performance claim for unbuilt hardware. The headline that survived a source-grounded forensic audit: what a compiler can recover from a flat capture, and the distinction between blocked-by-capture and blocked-by-proof, is itself the contribution.

2 · Headline results (each verified against source)

Primitive-set frontier — search SETS, not one tile

Best single primitive (gemv_lane_64) covers the worst workload at only 0.039; the best 2-set gemv_lane_64 + tile_8x16 lifts worst-workload coverage to 0.998 (macro 1.000).

Robust across pad-waste 5/10/20% and leave-one-out — but the specific pair is threshold-sensitive once finer tiles are added (linear-GEMM coverage only; attention excluded). A search-space construction claim, not a performance claim.

Operator concentration — two regimes

rdt: top op = 87% of MACs (depth-2 artifact — see §3).
pi05: 777 ops, only 17 distinct shapes; 34 ops to reach 50% MACs → instance concentration, not shape diversity.

Abstraction necessity (strict)

4 necessary · 5 useful · 11 possible · 7 blocked · 0 N/A

Not "everything supported". Low-bit abstractions correctly blocked; matrix_engine necessary for only 2/11 (corpus-narrow); skinny_gemm_or_gemv_engine necessary 9/11.

Capture fidelity — the central result

Preserved: op shapes, region roles, dtype, attention/softmax/norm (re-parsed from generics). Erased: the K-loop, KV-state across decode, packed-low-bit layout + scales.

Demonstrated from IR, not asserted: scf.for appears in only 1/11 captures (a gather artifact); no low-bit integer tensor types survive.

Residency & requirements envelope

Reload grows with K, resident is flat; int4 < int8 < bf16 capacity. Requirement envelopes (work ÷ deadline) over deadline/K/H grids — a requirement, never measured.

All K-dependent claims labeled configured/reference K (the loop is unrolled by export).

3 · Forensic verdict — source-grounded, one workload at a time

Every claim below cites a repo file:line / exported-FX op / MLIR snippet (full detail per workload in manual_validation/<wl>_source_to_mlir_audit.md).

Faithful

  • rdt 87% giant op is a real nn.Linear(2048,4096) over a 4096-token image context.
  • bitvla low-bit "blocked" is correct: source has real int2 BitLinear packing; export dequantizes it to f32.
  • xr0 3D-fold MACs are exact; pi05 "17 distinct shapes" confirmed correct (the doubt was a CSV misparse).

Bugs found & fixed

  • xr0 attention under-counted → SDPA-fused attention (prov.op=sdpa) was missed; recovered 0 → 14 ops, fraction 1.0 → 0.992.
  • groot attention over-counted → CategorySpecificMLP bmm mislabeled attention; 11 → 4 true + 7 batched_matmul (341M MACs separated).
  • xr0 K drift 10 → 5 (source num_steps=5).

Resolved with evidence (P20-S3)

  • rdt "one giant op" = confirmed depth-2 artifact: at depth-6 the top-op share drops 0.871 → 0.292 (cross-attn-KV is the dominant class ~88%, distributed, not one op).
  • "GEMV is capture-M": tiny_llama at true decode M=1 is also gemv_like (= the captured M=4) → the GEMV finding genuinely holds in the decode regime.

True, but state the caveat

  • All K / residency / loop claims rest on configured/assumed K (loop unrolled by torch.export).
  • Region roles are low-confidence in single-step diffusion captures.
  • Magnitudes are structural-only — only tiny_llama is a real checkpoint (and even it is truncated to 2 random layers).

Blocked-by-capture (needs frontend work, not analysis)

  • KV-state sizing, K-loop trip count, region-role recovery → need a loop/decode-preserving capture (torch.export unrolls loops — needs a TorchDynamo/torch.cond frontend).
  • Native low-bit datapath → the qdq capture is torchao-int8, not bitvla's native W1.58 ternary.

Tool E · Quant metadata

Real recaptures at high-level (named linalg_ext.softmax) + qdq (quant_ext.dequantize); loop-preserving is the documented torch.export-blocked frontier.

6 · What's next (deferred by design)

The highest-leverage remaining work is capture/frontend, not analysis:

  • Loop/decode-preserving capture — the single biggest unlock: K, KV-state, residency proof, region roles. Needs a TorchDynamo/torch.cond/scan frontend (torch.export unrolls loops).
  • Native-low-bit capture (packed ternary + scales) for bitvla's real W1.58 datapath.
  • Full true-decode M=1 recapture of the remaining AR workloads (loader edits; M=1 on tiny_llama already proves the regime).

Verification: 676/676 independent checks (verify_implementation.py) + ~178 pytest mechanism tests; every recovered MAC traces to an IR shape; no speedup/cycles/area/energy/optimality wording anywhere. Deliverables under merlin/benchmarks/dse_guidance/case_study/ (committed artifacts) and case_study/manual_validation/ (the forensic audit — start at final_judgment.md). Magnitudes are structural-only; rankings, ratios, shapes, and op-mix are the signal.
